The source frequency data is derived from OpenSubtitles2018:
http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php

Changes: removed frequency counts; kept only unique lowercase ASCII words
matching ^[a-z]+$; retained source frequency order; selected the first 5000
entries; converted to JSON. The 200- and 1000-word pools are prefixes of
this 5000-word pool. This is a subtitle-derived vocabulary, not a claim
to represent universal English word frequency.
