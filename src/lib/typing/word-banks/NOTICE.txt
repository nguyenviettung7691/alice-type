AliceType Vietnamese vocabulary data
===================================

vietnamese.json is adapted from FrequencyWords:
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
Source: content/2018/vi/vi_50k.txt
Revision: 525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6/content/2018/vi/vi_50k.txt
Retrieved: 2026-10-29
Content license: CC BY-SA 4.0, confirmed in the pinned upstream README:
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6/README.md
Underlying corpus: OpenSubtitles2018, http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php

Transformation: scripts/import-vietnamese-vocabulary.ts strips counts, NFC
normalizes, filters the supported lowercase Vietnamese/ASCII letter repertoire
and single-syllable spelling shape, allows at most one tone mark, excludes a
small explicit profanity list, deduplicates preserving source frequency order,
and retains the first 5000 entries. Smaller tiers are exact 200/1000 prefixes.
This is subtitle-derived whitespace-token practice data, not a dictionary or
universal frequency ranking. The automated shape filter is not a guarantee of
correct spelling or suitability; fluent Vietnamese editorial review is pending.
No manual/fluent review or exhaustive inappropriate-content filtering is claimed.

The Vietnamese data adaptation is CC BY-SA 4.0; the bundled license applies.
No endorsement is implied. Source data is fetched only by the maintainer import
script, never at application runtime.
